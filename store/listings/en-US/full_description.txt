■ Questions
Does the save editor work with my PC farm?
Yes. Copy the save folder from your PC to your phone, edit it, then copy it back. Stardew Valley 1.6 saves are supported.

What if an edit goes wrong?
Every edit starts with an automatic backup. Open Backups and restore the previous version.

Do I have to use the save editor?
No. The tracker and checklist work on their own. Tick items by hand or import them from a save.
