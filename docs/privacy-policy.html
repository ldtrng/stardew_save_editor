Tracker & Save Editor: Stardew Valley

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · App package: io.ld2t.stardew_save_editor

Tracker & Save Editor: Stardew Valley ("the app") is an unofficial, fan-made companion and save editor for the game Stardew Valley. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: your farm data stays on your device, and the only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob for advertising and Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and remote settings.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Tracker data – farms, checklists, calendar notes and settings you create are stored locally on your device.
  • Save files – a save file is read only when you choose it, or when you grant access to the Stardew Valley Saves folder. The app keeps backup copies of opened saves in its private storage so you can undo edits. Edited saves are written only to the location you choose.
  • This data is never uploaded by the app. It is removed when you use "Remove all data" or uninstall the app.

2. Information we do not collect

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location. We (the developer) do not operate servers that receive your data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit the frequency of and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Diagnostics and interaction data with ads (e.g. impressions, clicks, crashes of the SDK).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required) you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Usage analytics and remote settings (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, which features are used and how the app performs. It may collect:

  • App instance ID, Android advertising ID (where consent allows) and device information such as model, OS version, language and approximate (country-level) location;
  • App interactions such as screens opened, sessions, first open, ad impressions and feature events (for example "save exported" or "language changed").

The app does not send your save files, farm data, notes or any text you type to Analytics. Where consent is required, the choice you make in Settings › Ad privacy choices also applies to analytics storage. Firebase Remote Config is used to download app settings (such as how often ads may appear); it uses an installation ID only. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads, send usage statistics and download remote settings.
  • Folder access you grant (Android 12 and lower) – only to the folder you pick, to read and write your saves.
  • Advertising ID – used by AdMob and Firebase as described above.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the processing by Google AdMob and Google Firebase described above, no data is shared with third parties.

7. Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device and is deleted with the app or with Settings › Remove all data. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; analytics data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Backups you export are handled by the system file picker you choose.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
